Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect. Email, username, date of birth, gender, who you want to meet, city, bio, photo, swipes, matches, messages, reports and blocks.

How we use it. To run the app: show your profile to others, match you, deliver messages and live video, and keep the community safe.

Who sees it. Your profile (name, age, city, bio, photo) is visible to other adults using the app. Messages are visible only to you and the other person. Live rooms are visible to anyone who joins.

Sharing. We do not sell your data. Live video runs through our video provider, LiveKit.

Your control. You can edit your profile or permanently delete your account and data from Settings.

Security. Passwords are stored hashed. Connections use HTTPS.
